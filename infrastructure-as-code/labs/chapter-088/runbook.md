# Chapter 088 — Compliance SRE/DBRE runbooks

1. **Compliance incident:** classify, freeze unsafe change and assign accountable owner.
2. **Evidence tampering:** preserve source, quarantine report, recollect independently.
3. **OPA/Sentinel drift:** inspect deployed policy bundle, test deny paths, restore enforcement.
4. **IAM/OIDC:** verify exact trust, least privilege, access audit and revocation.
5. **Secrets exposure:** revoke/rotate, sanitize artifacts and preserve investigation logs.
6. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** verify admission, RBAC, AppProject/Flux scope and reconciliation.
7. **IaC state:** inspect encryption, KMS, versioning, lock, change ticket and backups.
8. **Cloud audit:** restore AWS/Azure/GCP logging, document gaps and retention.
9. **DBRE restore:** execute isolated PITR, validate KMS, RPO/RTO, SLO and data integrity.
10. **Exception:** scope, owner, independent approver, compensating control, expiry.
11. **Supply chain:** validate signer, SBOM, dependency pins, scan age and provenance.
12. **Auditor handoff:** evidence period, chain of custody, exceptions and signed closure.
