# Chapter 066 — CloudFormation Foundations Runbooks

## R1 Identity and preflight
Verify account/region/caller, scoped execution role, template, quotas and resource ownership.

## R2 Change-set review
Inspect Add/Modify/Remove, replacement, IAM, parameters, dependencies and approvals.

## R3 Rollback failure
Review stack events, preserve evidence, avoid force deletion and plan recovery.

## R4 Drift
Capture drift report, determine owner and reconcile via reviewed change set.

## R5 Stateful data
Verify RDS backup/PITR, encryption, retention, replica health and restore.

## R6 IAM incident
Revoke broad role, audit CloudTrail, re-scope and validate.

## R7 StackSet failure
Review account/region targets, permissions and partial results.

## R8 Cost and cleanup
Audit retained/orphaned resources and tag ownership before deletion.

## R9 Post-change
Verify stack status, CloudWatch SLO, backup, drift and application health.
