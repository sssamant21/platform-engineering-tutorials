# Chapter 090 — Enterprise testing, compliance and drift runbooks

1. **Incident command:** severity, blast radius, owner and containment.
2. **Supply chain/OIDC:** freeze promotion, revoke identities, verify provenance.
3. **Terraform/OpenTofu:** preserve state, inspect plan/delete/replace and backup.
4. **Pulumi/CFN/Bicep:** validate preview, change sets and actual cloud attributes.
5. **Terratest/Molecule:** isolate account, driver, credentials and teardown.
6. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** admission, RBAC, DNS, AppProject/Flux source and rollback.
7. **Cloud IAM/policy:** verify AWS/Azure/GCP org controls, deny paths and audit.
8. **Audit:** hash, trusted signer, timestamp, retention and independent recollection.
9. **PostgreSQL/RDS/Aurora:** PITR, WAL, KMS, consistency, measured RPO/RTO.
10. **MongoDB/Redis/Kafka/OpenSearch:** quorum, ACL, ISR, snapshot and failover.
11. **Cost/cleanup:** TTL, owner tags, orphans and zero-owned-resource proof.
12. **Acceptance:** SLO, independent approvals, exceptions and corrective action.
