# Chapter 069 — Azure Bicep Advanced Operations Runbooks

## R1 Target identity
Verify tenant, subscription, resource group, workload identity and RBAC.

## R2 Module supply chain
Check module registry, version, digest, CI provenance and approval.

## R3 Deployment-stack lifecycle
Review managed resources, deny settings, detach/delete policy and stateful ownership.

## R4 What-if
Inspect additions, modifications, removals, unresolved expressions and scope.

## R5 Partial multi-subscription rollout
Freeze further targets, capture successes/failures, reconcile and reapprove.

## R6 DBRE recovery
Verify PITR, backups, replication, SLO, RPO/RTO and restore evidence.

## R7 Security incident
Revoke identity, rotate secrets, audit Activity Log and quarantine artifacts.

## R8 Drift
Review manual changes and reconcile through approved source of truth.

## R9 FinOps
Review retained resources, tags, budgets, quotas and owner.

## R10 Post-change
Verify Azure Monitor, deployments, backup, policy and handover.
