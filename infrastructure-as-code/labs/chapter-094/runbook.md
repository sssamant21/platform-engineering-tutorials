# Chapter 094 — IaC SLO and reliability runbooks

1. **Incident command:** severity, service tier, commander, impact and blast radius.
2. **SLI:** verify denominator, freshness, metric provenance and missing data.
3. **Error budget:** objective, windows, burn rate, sample floor and escalation.
4. **Alerting:** Prometheus rules, Alertmanager routes, silence, inhibition and test delivery.
5. **IaC:** Terraform/OpenTofu plan, immutable artifact, policy and change freeze.
6. **State/provider:** backend lease, version, API throttling and rollback safety.
7. **Canary:** service SLO, stop/rollback, independent approval and cooldown.
8. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** Argo CD/Flux, admission, RBAC, networking and storage.
9. **Cloud IAM:** AWS/Azure/GCP identity, OIDC, policy enforcement and audit.
10. **PostgreSQL/Aurora/MongoDB/Redis:** PITR, quorum, ACL, restore and measured RPO/RTO.
11. **Kafka/OpenSearch/Elasticsearch/Snowflake:** ISR, shards, snapshots, CDC and data correctness.
12. **Closure:** post-change SLO, zero orphans, evidence, incident report and sign-off.
