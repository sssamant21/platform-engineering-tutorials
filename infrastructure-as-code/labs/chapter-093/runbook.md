# Chapter 093 — IaC incident correlation and RCA runbooks

1. **Incident command:** severity, affected service, commander, owner and blast radius.
2. **Change correlation:** protected Git, CI, immutable plan, actor and cloud audit.
3. **State:** preserve Terraform/OpenTofu backend version, lock, provider and safe rollback.
4. **Other IaC:** Pulumi, CloudFormation, Bicep and Ansible native evidence.
5. **Telemetry:** OTel context, Prometheus freshness, dropped events and UTC ordering.
6. **SLO:** burn rate, error budget, paging route, suppression and escalation.
7. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** admission, RBAC, Argo CD/Flux, storage and networking.
8. **Cloud IAM:** AWS/Azure/GCP permissions, OIDC, policy mode and logs.
9. **PostgreSQL/Aurora:** PITR, WAL, KMS, replication, measured RPO/RTO.
10. **MongoDB/Redis/Kafka/OpenSearch/Snowflake:** quorum, ACL, ISR, snapshot, grants, CDC and recovery.
11. **Change failure:** alternatives, causal uncertainty, mitigation, canary and rollback.
12. **Postmortem:** verified timeline, independent evidence, corrective owners and sign-off.
