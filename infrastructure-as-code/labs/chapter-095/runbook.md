# Chapter 095 — Enterprise reliability acceptance runbooks

1. **Incident command:** declare severity, owner, target and freeze unsafe automation.
2. **Telemetry:** OTel/Prometheus health, freshness, provenance and missing-signal UNKNOWN.
3. **SLO:** denominator, error budget, paired-window burn rate, paging receipt and escalation.
4. **Terraform/OpenTofu:** plan digest, backend version, lock, provider and rollback review.
5. **Other IaC:** Pulumi, CloudFormation, Bicep and Ansible native audit.
6. **Cloud:** AWS/Azure/GCP IAM, audit, inventory pagination and policy enforcement.
7. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** Argo CD/Flux ownership, admission, networking and storage.
8. **Drift/RCA:** desired/managed/actual, change actor, competing hypotheses, time skew.
9. **PostgreSQL/Aurora/RDS:** WAL, PITR, KMS, failover, data integrity and RPO/RTO.
10. **MongoDB/Redis/Kafka:** quorum, ACL, ISR, persistence, backup and recovery.
11. **Elasticsearch/OpenSearch/Snowflake:** shards, snapshots, CDC, DR and data correctness.
12. **Acceptance:** live SLO, page delivery, zero orphans, immutable evidence, postmortem and independent sign-off.
