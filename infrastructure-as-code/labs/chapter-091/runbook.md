# Chapter 091 — IaC observability SRE/DBRE runbooks

1. **Deployment failure:** correlate CI, source, plan, policy, provider, change and rollback.
2. **State contention:** inspect lock owner, backend latency and parallel jobs.
3. **Cloud audit:** verify AWS/Azure/GCP logs, ingestion lag and IAM.
4. **OpenTelemetry:** collector health, dropped spans, exporter TLS and queue.
5. **Prometheus:** scrape freshness, cardinality, OOM and retention.
6. **SLO alerts:** denominator, burn rate, routing, suppression and error budget.
7. **Kubernetes/GitOps:** admission, RBAC, Argo CD/Flux, DNS and storage.
8. **PostgreSQL/Aurora:** WAL, PITR, KMS, replication, measured RPO/RTO.
9. **MongoDB/Redis/Kafka:** elections, cache, ACL, ISR, lag and failover.
10. **OpenSearch/Snowflake:** shards, IOPS, latency, queue and CDC.
11. **Drift/compliance:** observation age, policy denials, safe reconciliation.
12. **Closure:** validate fresh telemetry, SLO, restore, evidence and sign-off.
